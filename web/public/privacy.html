Cab Books privacy notice

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Who we are

Cab Books is run by All Black Taxis, 57 Upfield Road, London W7 1AW. We are the data controller for the personal information held in Cab Books. Contact us about your data at marcin@allblacktaxis.co.uk.

What we hold

  • Your sign-in email address and an encrypted password (handled by Google Firebase Authentication – we never see your password).
  • What you choose to record: journeys, fares, tips, fees, expenses, receipts, statements and bank lines you import, your calendar entries, and your settings.
  • Details you add for receipts, such as your name, phone number, email and taxi licence number, and the names of customers you issue receipts to.
  • Photos of receipts you choose to take with the Receipt photo button. They are stored with your books and only you can see them.
  • Suggestions you post in the shared suggestions box (other drivers can read these).

If you tap the location button on the taxi ranks or toilets map, your phone uses your location to show nearby places. Your location stays on your phone; it is not saved in your books or sent to us.

Why we hold it

To provide the Cab Books service you sign up for: keeping your books, tracking your turnover against the VAT threshold, and producing your reports and receipts. Our lawful basis is the contract between us (providing the service you asked for). We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or share it with other drivers.

Who can see it

Your books are private to your account. Other drivers cannot see them. The database is set up so that only you can read or change your records. Our service provider, Google (Firebase), stores the data for us on servers in the United Kingdom (London). Google acts only on our instructions.

Cab Books' two administrators (All Black Taxis) can see account details only: your sign-in email, the name you entered, when you signed up and last used Cab Books, the type of phone or computer, how you use it (Full books or Just tracking), whether train alerts are on, and your plan. They cannot see your jobs, earnings, expenses, receipts or anything else in your books.

How long we keep it

For as long as you keep your account. HMRC expects business records to be kept for at least 5 years after the 31 January submission deadline of the relevant tax year, so we recommend exporting your records before you close your account. When you ask us to delete your account, we delete your records within 30 days.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, ask us to correct or delete it, or object to how we use it. Email marcin@allblacktaxis.co.uk. If you are unhappy with how we handle your data you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections and protected by Google's security. Use a strong password that you don't use anywhere else.
