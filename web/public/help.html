Getting started with Cab Books

Your income, expenses and VAT in one place, made for London cab drivers. This takes about 10 minutes.

🔒 Your information is safe with us

Only you can see your books: your jobs, your earnings, your bank statements, all of it. We don’t look at them, and we never sell or share them.

Open Cab Books

Cab Books opens in a new tab. Keep this page open and come back to it after each step. You can also find this guide in Cab Books under Settings.

1. Sign up

  1. Open Cab Books using the button above, or the link you were sent.
  2. Tap New here? Create an account.
  3. Type your email and choose a password of at least 8 characters. Tap Create account.
  4. Cab Books sends you an email to check your address. Open it and tap the link (check your junk folder if it doesn't arrive).

Only you can see your books.

2. Put it on your home screen

Do this once and Cab Books opens from an icon like any other app. No App Store needed.

This is the icon you’ll see on your phone’s home screen. Tap it whenever you want to use Cab Books.

iPhone

  1. Open Cab Books in Safari (the compass icon).
  2. Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow pointing up). It’s at the bottom of the screen, or behind the ••• button on newer iPhones.
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  4. Go back to your phone’s home screen. You’ll see the Cab Books icon. Tap it to open Cab Books.

Android

  1. Open Cab Books in Chrome.
  2. Tap the three dots at the top right.
  3. Tap Add to Home screen (some phones say Install app), then Add or Install.
  4. Go back to your phone’s home screen. You’ll see the Cab Books icon. Tap it to open Cab Books.

Always open Cab Books from this icon, not from a Safari tab or bookmark. On iPhone, train alerts only work from the icon.

Computer

Bookmark the page. On a computer you get a wider view, which is handy for reports and your tax year.

3. Set it up

The first time you sign in, Cab Books asks three things:

  1. Your first name. It’s shown as the driver on receipts you give customers.
  2. How you’d like to use it.
    • Just tracking: you type in your jobs and expenses yourself, and Cab Books shows your VAT position and reports. These are based only on what you’ve typed in, so you’ll still need to check them against your bank statements and the statements from the driver apps you use. Driver apps such as FreeNow, Gett and Uber report your earnings to HMRC. Cab Books doesn’t send anything to HMRC.
    • Full books: everything in Just tracking, plus you bring in your bank statements and driver app statements. Cab Books checks them against your jobs, so you can spot anything missing and your figures line up with what the driver apps report to HMRC.

    Not sure which to pick? Start with Just tracking to get used to Cab Books, then move to Full books when you’re ready. You can switch between them at any time in Settings → How you use Cab Books, and nothing you’ve put in is lost.

  3. The first month you’re recording. This is the month you start putting your jobs into Cab Books. Your VAT position and tax figures are worked out from this month onwards, so it’s important to get it right.

    Drove before then? The VAT limit is based on your takings over the last 12 months, so Cab Books will ask you for a total for each month before you started. Tap Add my takings on the Position screen, or go to Settings → VAT settings → Add or change earlier takings. You only need one total per month, not every job.

Cab Books can record jobs from all the main hailing apps and card machines. Open Settings (the cog at the top) → Providers I use:

  • Job apps: tick the apps you currently use, for example FreeNow, Gett, Uber or Curb Flow, so only those buttons show when you add a job.
  • Card machine in my cab: tick the one you take card payments with (Curb, HIPS, Helix Pay or Taxiworld). It then shows under Paid by when you add a job, for example “Curb card”, and its fee is taken off card fares for you.

Card machine not listed? Tick Other card, type your machine’s name in the box that appears and tap Save name. Then put its fee in Settings → Fee defaults. If you’d rather we added it for you, tell us in the suggestions box in Settings.

Bottom bar (also in Settings): tick the buttons you want along the bottom of the screen, for example Today, Position, Fare calculator and Trains. To change their order, hold a button on the bar and slide it left or right.

4. Turn on train alerts

Cab Books can tell you when trains are being cancelled at the London terminals, or when the Heathrow or Gatwick Express is down, so you know where the work is.

  1. Put Cab Books on your home screen first (step 2) and open it from the icon. iPhones need iOS 16.4 or later.
  2. Go to Settings → Train alerts and tap Turn on alerts on this phone.
  3. Tap Allow when your phone asks about notifications.

Sensible alerts are already switched on (for example 4 or more cancellations at a station, or an airport express down). You can change them in the same place if you want.

On iPhone an alert can occasionally arrive a little late. When Cab Books is open, a banner at the top shows the same warning straight away. Live counts for every terminal are on the Trains screen. Add the Trains button to your bottom bar in Settings → Bottom bar.

5. Bring in your bank statement

This is the quickest way to get your books up to date. Cab Books spots payments from FreeNow, Gett, Uber, Curb, HIPS, Helix Pay, Taxiworld and ComCab, adds back the commission to work out your fares, and sorts your spending (rent, fuel, charging, airport drop-offs and so on).

Get the file from your bank

  1. Log in to your bank's online banking website. A computer is usually easiest, as many banking apps can't download files.
  2. Open the account your taxi money goes into and look for Download, Export or Statements.
  3. Choose the dates (the last 3 months is a good start) and the format CSV. If there's a choice, pick "CSV" or "Excel (CSV)", not PDF.
  4. Save the file. If you did this on a computer, you can bring it in on the computer too, since your books are the same everywhere you sign in.

Bring it into Cab Books

  1. Make sure you're on Full books (Settings → How you use Cab Books).
  2. Go to the Import tab and find Bank statement.
  3. Tap Choose file, pick your CSV, then tap Read it.
  4. Check the columns look right (date, description, money in, money out). They're usually picked for you.
  5. Under Needs a decision, choose a category for any payee Cab Books doesn't know. Anything you leave is treated as personal. Your choices are remembered next time.
  6. Tap any category in the summary to see every line in it. Move a single line, or a whole payee, to a different category (for example personal Amazon orders). On your first bank statement, tap Looks right on each business category before Import is allowed.
  7. Tap Import.

It's safe to bring in the same statement twice, or statements that overlap. Lines already in are skipped.

Do this every week. Pick dates from your last download to today: overlapping is fine.

For every job, tip and commission, also bring in the statements from your taxi apps. See 6. App statements.

6. Statements from your taxi apps

Your bank shows the money that reached you. The apps' statements add the detail: each job, the tip and the commission taken. They all go in one place: the Import tab, under Taxi app statements.

  • You can choose several files at once, from different apps.
  • Cab Books skips anything it has already read, so downloading the same week twice does no harm.
  • Statements replace the bank estimates for the same weeks automatically. Nothing is counted twice.

What to get, and when

  • Bank: CSV, every week (see 5. Bank statement).
  • FreeNow: 3 PDFs, every week after Tuesday's payment.
  • Gett: PDF, every week (or wait for the quarterly email).
  • Uber: PDF, every week (or at month end).
  • Curb (card payments in the cab): Excel, every month.

FreeNow

  1. Log in to the FreeNow driver portal and go to Invoices.
  2. Find the week. Its Select file box has three separate files. Download each one: Invoice summary.pdf, Tour overview.pdf and Invoice.pdf.
  3. In Cab Books, go to Import → Taxi app statements and choose all three together.

Gett

  1. In the Gett driver app, tap the three bars at the top, then Driver portal.
  2. Go to Accounting and choose Daily, Weekly or Annual statements.
  3. Tap the date and download the PDF (or have it emailed to you).
  4. In Cab Books, go to Import → Taxi app statements and choose the files.

Gett also emails you statements every quarter. If you'd rather not download them each week, wait for those. Daily, weekly and quarterly statements can overlap: each job is only counted once.

Uber

  1. Go to drivers.uber.com and sign in.
  2. Go to Earnings → Statements.
  3. Pick the week and tap Download.
  4. In Cab Books, go to Import → Taxi app statements and choose the files. You can do several weeks at once.

An Uber week runs Monday 4am to Monday 4am, and the money reaches your bank about two days later. Cab Books also reads Uber's earnings CSV if you have that instead.

Curb (card payments in your cab)

  1. Go to the Curb eFleet driver portal: efleet.gocurb.com.
  2. Login ID: your badge number. Password: your Curb portal password. Select group: UK London.
  3. Open Credit card transactions, choose last month's dates and export to Excel.
  4. In Cab Books, go to Import → Taxi app statements and choose the file.

FreeNow or Gett jobs that the passenger paid by card through Curb are already sorted out for you, so they won't be counted twice.

Your weekly routine (about 10 minutes)

  1. On Tuesday or Wednesday, download your bank CSV, FreeNow's 3 PDFs, Gett's statements and Uber's weekly statement.
  2. Import → Bank statement, then Import → Taxi app statements.
  3. Have a look at Position to see where you are against the VAT limit.

At month end

  1. Bring in last month's Curb report.
  2. Go to Data → Monthly checks and mark each source Checked once its statements are in.

7. Everyday use

You’re ready to go. Now that you’ve set up your apps and train alerts, you can start adding your fares. You can add every kind of job:

  • Hailing apps: FreeNow, Gett, Uber and the others you ticked
  • Street jobs: tap Hand
  • Private pre-books: tap Private
  • Cash jobs: pick Cash under Paid by

Everything you add to Cab Books, including cash jobs, is kept as part of your business records and included in your tax and VAT figures. Remember that you’re responsible for your own tax return.

Adding a job: an example

You pick up a street job. The fare is £10, paid by card, and they add a £2 tip.

▶ Watch a short video
  1. Tap Today on the bottom bar.
  2. Under Add job, tap Hand (a street job).
  3. Under Paid by, tap your card machine, for example Curb card.
  4. Type the fare and the tip. Amounts go in pence, like a till: type 1000 for £10.00 and 200 for £2.00. There’s no need for a dot.
  5. Tap Save job. Cab Books works out the card fee and shows what you keep.

Other jobs work the same way: pick the app, then Paid by Account (paid in the app), card or Cash. Tap an extras button for drop-off fees or parking. Busy shift? Tap 🎤 Say jobs and say several at once, for example “FreeNow account £50, Uber £62, petrol £40”. You check them before they’re saved.

🛈 About tips

Tips aren’t part of your VAT turnover, but they are taxable income. Cab Books keeps them separate for you.

Your other screens

  • Position: how this month is going and where you are against the VAT limit. You’ll get a warning well before you get close.
  • Reports: month by month, the tax year, the quarter (for Making Tax Digital), days worked and days off, and a pack for your accountant.
  • Handy screens: Fares (fare calculator with the route on a map), Tonight (when football, concerts and West End shows let out), Flights, Ranks, Toilets, Trains and Tube. Add the ones you want to your bottom bar in Settings → Bottom bar.

8. Receipt photos

Keep a photo of every receipt for your accountant and HMRC.

  1. On Today, tap Snap a receipt, take the photo, check the amount and type, and tap Save.
  2. Paid by card? (Full books only) That’s all. When you bring in your bank statement, the photo links itself to the payment.
  3. Paid cash? Use Add an expense instead, choose Cash under Paid by, then tap Add receipt photo, so the cost is counted.

At the end of the tax year, Reports → Tax year makes one PDF of all your receipt photos to send your accountant.

9. Fixing mistakes

  • Wrong job? Tap Edit on it in Today or the Log.
  • Commission changed? Settings → Fee defaults → Rate changes: add the new rate and the date it starts. Earlier jobs keep the old rate.
  • Typed a batch of jobs under the wrong app or payment? Settings → Move or re-price jobs: pick what they were typed in as, the dates and what they should be. It shows you the change before you confirm.
  • Want to practise first? Settings → Practice and clearing up: switch practice on, and jobs you type are never counted. You can delete them all afterwards.
