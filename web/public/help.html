Getting started with Cab Books

Your income, expenses and VAT in one place, made for London cab drivers. This takes about 10 minutes.

🔒 Your information is safe with us

Only you can see your books: your jobs, your earnings, your bank statements, all of it. We don’t look at them, and we never sell or share them.

Open Cab Books

Cab Books opens in a new tab. Keep this page open and come back to it after each step. You can also find this guide in Cab Books under Settings → Help and suggestions.

1. Sign up

  1. Open Cab Books using the button above, or the link you were sent.
  2. Tap New here? Create an account.
  3. Type your email and choose a password of at least 8 characters. Tap Create account.
  4. Cab Books sends you an email to check your address. Open it and tap the link (check your junk folder if it doesn't arrive).

Only you can see your books.

2. Put it on your home screen

Do this once and Cab Books opens from an icon like any other app. No App Store needed.

This is the icon you’ll see on your phone’s home screen. Tap it whenever you want to use Cab Books.

iPhone

  1. Open Cab Books in Safari (the compass icon).
  2. Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow pointing up). It’s at the bottom of the screen, or behind the ••• button on newer iPhones.
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  4. Go back to your phone’s home screen. You’ll see the Cab Books icon. Tap it to open Cab Books.

Android

  1. Open Cab Books in Chrome.
  2. Tap the three dots at the top right.
  3. Tap Add to Home screen (some phones say Install app), then Add or Install.
  4. Go back to your phone’s home screen. You’ll see the Cab Books icon. Tap it to open Cab Books.

Always open Cab Books from this icon, not from a Safari tab or bookmark. On iPhone, train alerts only work from the icon.

Computer

Bookmark the page. On a computer you get a wider view, which is handy for reports and your tax year.

3. Set it up

The first time you sign in, Cab Books asks your first name (shown as the driver on receipts) and the first month you’re recording. Then it shows Quick setup: one page of questions. Tick what applies to you and the app sets itself up. You don’t have to read this guide first.

  1. How do you want to keep your books?
    • Basic: you type in your fares and expenses yourself, and Cab Books tracks your earnings, VAT and tax from what you enter. These figures are based only on what you’ve typed in, so you’ll still need to check them against your bank statements and the statements from the driver apps you use. Driver apps such as FreeNow, Gett and Uber report your earnings to HMRC. Cab Books doesn’t send anything to HMRC.
    • Full: everything in Basic, plus you bring in your bank and driver app statements. Cab Books checks every payment against your bank and closes each month, so you can spot anything missing and your figures line up with what the driver apps report to HMRC.

    Not sure which to pick? Start with Basic to get used to Cab Books, then move to Full when you’re ready. You can move from Basic to Full later without losing anything.

  2. Which job apps and accounts do you work for? FreeNow, Gett, Uber, ComCab and so on. Only those buttons show when you add a job. Street and Private are always there.
  3. Which card payment provider do you use? Curb, ComCab, Taxiworld, Helix Pay, SumUp or Other, and its fee, so what you keep from each card fare is right.

    Card machine not listed? Tick Other and type its name and fee. If you’d rather we added it to the list, tell us through Settings → Help and suggestions.

  4. Are you registered for VAT? No, getting close, or yes.
  5. Were you driving before you started using Cab Books? The VAT limit is based on your takings over the last 12 months. If you tick Yes, tap Add my earlier takings and enter one total for each month before you started. You don’t need every job, just one total per month.
  6. Do you send quarterly updates to HMRC (Making Tax Digital)? If you’re not sure, Cab Books works it out from your takings.
  7. Your cab: rented, on finance or owned, and whether it’s electric and you charge at home.
  8. Regular expenses, such as your union, insurance, phone and accountant. Enter each amount once and it’s added to your expenses for you.
  9. What would you like on your bottom bar? Tick the buttons you want. To change the order later, press and hold a button on the bar and slide it.
  10. Which phone alerts would you like? Train delays, events finishing and tax payment dates.
  11. Reminders to bring in your statements (Full only), on the day and time you choose.
  12. Your receipts (optional): the name, phone and licence number printed on receipts.

Press Finish setup and you’ll see a summary of what was set up, with the guides you need.

To change one thing later, go to Settings (the cog at the top) → Changes to your setup. To go through everything again, open Quick setup in Settings. Nothing you’ve already recorded is changed or deleted.

Regular expenses are under Settings → Changes to your setup → Your cab and regular expenses. There you can Pause one for a holiday, or Change amount from a date. Days before that date keep the old amount.

4. Turn on train alerts

Cab Books can tell you when trains are being cancelled at the London terminals, or when the Heathrow or Gatwick Express is down, so you know where the work is.

  1. Put Cab Books on your home screen first (step 2) and open it from the icon. iPhones need iOS 16.4 or later.
  2. Go to Settings → Changes to your setup → Alerts and reminders and tap Turn on alerts on this phone.
  3. Tap Allow when your phone asks about notifications.

Sensible alerts are already switched on (for example 4 or more cancellations at a station, or an airport express down). You can change them in the same place if you want.

On iPhone an alert can occasionally arrive a little late. When Cab Books is open, a banner at the top shows the same warning straight away. Live counts for every terminal are on the Trains screen. Add the Trains button to your bottom bar in Settings → Changes to your setup → Bottom bar.

5. Everyday use

You’re ready to go. Now that you’ve set up your apps and train alerts, you can start adding your fares. You can add every kind of job:

  • Hailing apps: FreeNow, Gett, Uber and the others you ticked
  • Street jobs: tap Street
  • Private pre-books: tap Private
  • Cash jobs: pick Cash under Paid by

Everything you add to Cab Books, including cash jobs, is kept as part of your business records and included in your tax and VAT figures. Remember that you’re responsible for your own tax return.

1. Adding a job (Today)

Example: you pick up a street job. The fare is £10, paid by card, and they add a £2 tip.

▶ Watch a short video
  1. Tap Today on the bottom bar.
  2. Under Add job, tap Street.
  3. Under Paid by, tap your card machine, for example Curb card.
  4. Type the fare and the tip. Amounts go in pence, like a till: type 1000 for £10.00 and 200 for £2.00. There’s no need for a dot.
  5. Tap Save job. Cab Books works out the card fee and shows what you keep.

Other jobs work the same way: pick the app (or Street or Private), then Paid by Account (paid in the app), card or Cash. Tap an extras button for drop-off fees or parking. Busy shift? Tap 🎤 Say jobs and say several at once, for example “FreeNow account £50, Uber £62, petrol £40”. You check them before they’re saved.

🛈 About tips

Tips aren’t part of your VAT turnover, but they are taxable income. Cab Books keeps them separate for you.

2. Position: how you’re doing against the VAT limit

Tap Position on the bottom bar to see how this month is going and where you are against the VAT limit. You’ll get a warning well before you get close.

▶ Watch a short video

From the top of the screen:

  • Rolling 12 months: how much more you can take before you reach the £90,000 VAT line. The bar is green under 80%, amber from 80% and red from 90%.
  • This month: what you’ve taken so far, and how much a week you can take until month end.
  • Earnings chart and Last 12 months: each month’s takings and the running 12-month total.
  • You can earn and Room in the coming months: how much you can take in each of the next few months and still stay under the VAT line.
  • Tax to put aside: a rough guide to your income tax and National Insurance, how much to put aside, and when HMRC payments are due.

3. Reports: your earnings, costs and profit

Tap Reports on the bottom bar. Along the top, choose the period: Today, Week, Month, Quarter, Tax year, 12 months or Custom dates. Use ‹ and › to go back and forward.

▶ Watch a short video

For the period you pick, you’ll see:

  • What you took, with tips shown separately, your VAT turnover (no tips), commission and fees, expenses and rent, and your profit. Tap VAT view, no tips to see the figures without tips.
  • Days worked and days off.
  • How each provider performed: what you kept from every £100 of fares with each app, so you can see which work pays best.
  • Quarter: the figures for your quarterly update for HMRC (Making Tax Digital), as a PDF or spreadsheet.
  • Tax year: month-by-month charts, a tax estimate with payment dates, and For your accountant: a PDF or spreadsheet of your year, plus one PDF of all your receipt photos.

  • Tonight: when West End shows, concerts, football and big events at venues like The O2 let out, today or tomorrow. A time with a ? is the usual finishing time, not a confirmed one.

  • Flights: arrivals at Heathrow and London City, hour by hour, with how many big planes are due. Passengers reach the rank roughly 30 to 60 minutes after landing.

  • Trains: cancellations in the next 2 hours at each London terminal. Tap a count to see which trains.

  • Tube: the status of every line. Tap a line to see what the problem is.

  • Fares: the fare calculator. Type the pick-up and drop-off, or tap the map, to see the route and the estimated fare.
  • Ranks and Toilets: maps of taxi ranks (including rest ranks) and toilets near you.

6. Receipt photos

Keep a photo of every receipt for your accountant and HMRC.

  1. On Today, tap Snap a receipt, take the photo, check the amount and type, and tap Save.
  2. Paid by card? (Full only) That’s all. When you bring in your bank statement, the photo links itself to the payment.
  3. Paid cash? Use Add an expense instead, choose Cash under Paid by, then tap Add receipt photo, so the cost is counted.

At the end of the tax year, Reports → Tax year makes one PDF of all your receipt photos to send your accountant.

7. Fixing mistakes

▶ Watch a short video
  • Wrong job? Tap Edit on it in Today or the Log, change it and tap Update job. To remove it, tap Edit, then Delete this job, then Tap again to delete.
  • Commission changed? Settings → Changes to your setup → Fees and commission → Rate changes. Add the new rate and the date it starts. Earlier jobs keep the old rate.
  • Typed a batch of jobs under the wrong app or payment, or at an old rate? Settings → Changes to your setup → Fees and commission → Move or re-price jobs. Pick what they were typed in as, the dates and what they should be, then tap Check which jobs. You see the change before you confirm. Jobs from statements and bank imports aren’t changed.
  • Want to practise first? Settings → Changes to your setup → Basic or Full, and practice mode → Practice mode. Jobs and expenses you type while it’s on show on Today and in the Log, but never count towards VAT, tax or reports. Afterwards you can tick them and delete them.
  • Keep a copy, just in case: Settings → Account details → Save a backup of all my books.

Still stuck? Email us from Settings → Help and suggestions.

8. Bring in your bank statement

🔒 Your bank statement stays private. Only you can see it, and nobody at Cab Books looks at it.

For drivers on Full. This is the quickest way to get your books up to date. Cab Books spots payments from FreeNow, Gett, Uber, Curb, ComCab, Taxiworld and Helix Pay, adds back the commission to work out your fares, and sorts your spending (rent, fuel, charging, airport drop-offs and so on).

Get the file from your bank

  1. Log in to your bank's online banking website. A computer is usually easiest, as many banking apps can't download files.
  2. Open the account your taxi money goes into and look for Download, Export or Statements.
  3. Choose the dates (the last 3 months is a good start) and the format CSV or Excel. Don’t choose PDF, because Cab Books can’t read it.
  4. Save the file. If you did this on a computer, you can bring it in on the computer too, since your books are the same everywhere you sign in.

Bring it into Cab Books

  1. Make sure you’re on Full (Settings → Changes to your setup → Basic or Full).
  2. Go to the Import tab and find Bank statement.
  3. Tap Choose your bank statement file, pick the file, then tap Read it.
  4. Check the columns look right (date, description, money in, money out). They're usually picked for you.
  5. Under Needs a decision, choose a category for any payee Cab Books doesn't know. Anything you leave is treated as personal. Your choices are remembered next time.
  6. Tap any category in the summary to see every line in it. Move a single line, or a whole payee, to a different category (for example personal Amazon orders). On your first bank statement, tap Looks right on each business category before Import is allowed.
  7. Tap Import.

Your bank statement becomes the record of your spending. Expenses you typed in yourself are replaced by the bank’s figures, up to the last date on the statement, so nothing is counted twice.

It's safe to bring in the same statement twice, or statements that overlap. Lines already in are skipped.

Do this once a month, for the month before. Your statement reminder will nudge you. Pick dates from your last download to today: overlapping is fine.

For every job, tip and commission, also bring in the statements from your taxi apps. See 9. App statements.

9. Statements from your taxi apps

For drivers on Full. Your bank shows the money that reached you. The apps' statements add the detail: each job, the tip and the commission taken. They all go in one place: the Import tab, under Taxi app statements and Heathrow drop-offs. Tap Choose your files (PDF, Excel or CSV).

  • You can choose several files at once, from different apps.
  • Cab Books skips anything it has already read, so downloading the same week twice does no harm.
  • Statements replace the bank estimates for the same weeks automatically. Nothing is counted twice.

What to get, and when

  • Bank: CSV, once a month for last month (see 8. Bank statement).
  • FreeNow: 3 PDFs, every week after Tuesday's payment.
  • Gett: PDF, every week (or wait for the quarterly email).
  • Uber: PDF, every week (or at month end).
  • Curb (card payments in the cab): Excel, every month.

FreeNow

  1. Log in to the FreeNow driver portal and go to Invoices.
  2. Find the week. Its Select file box has three separate files. Download each one: Invoice summary.pdf, Tour overview.pdf and Invoice.pdf.
  3. In Cab Books, go to Import → Taxi app statements and Heathrow drop-offs and choose all three together.

Gett

  1. In the Gett driver app, tap the three bars at the top, then Driver portal.
  2. Go to Accounting and choose Daily, Weekly or Annual statements.
  3. Tap the date and download the PDF (or have it emailed to you).
  4. In Cab Books, go to Import → Taxi app statements and Heathrow drop-offs and choose the files.

Gett also emails you statements every quarter. If you'd rather not download them each week, wait for those. Daily, weekly and quarterly statements can overlap: each job is only counted once.

Uber

  1. Go to drivers.uber.com and sign in.
  2. Go to Earnings → Statements.
  3. Pick the week and tap Download.
  4. In Cab Books, go to Import → Taxi app statements and Heathrow drop-offs and choose the files. You can do several weeks at once.

An Uber week runs Monday 4am to Monday 4am, and the money reaches your bank about two days later. Cab Books also reads Uber's earnings CSV if you have that instead.

Curb (card payments in your cab)

  1. Go to the Curb eFleet driver portal: efleet.gocurb.com.
  2. Login ID: your badge number. Password: your Curb portal password. Select group: UK London.
  3. Open Credit card transactions, choose last month's dates and export to Excel.
  4. In Cab Books, go to Import → Taxi app statements and Heathrow drop-offs and choose the file.

FreeNow or Gett jobs that the passenger paid by card in your cab (on your card machine) are already sorted out for you, so they won't be counted twice.

Heathrow drop-offs

Only if you pay for Heathrow drop-offs by AutoPay from a card that isn’t in your bank import. If they come out of the bank account you import, skip this.

  1. Go to heathrow.com/dropoff and sign in (personal or business account).
  2. Open your trip or payment history and download it as CSV or Excel.
  3. In Cab Books, go to Import → Taxi app statements and Heathrow drop-offs and choose the file. Each paid drop-off is added as a Parking / drop-off cost.

Every week (about 10 minutes)

  1. On Tuesday or Wednesday, download FreeNow’s 3 PDFs, Gett’s statements and Uber’s weekly statement.
  2. Go to Import → Taxi app statements and Heathrow drop-offs and choose them all together.
  3. Have a look at Position to see where you are against the VAT limit.

Every month

  1. Bring in your bank statement (see 8. Bank statement) and last month’s Curb report.
  2. Go to Data → Monthly checks and mark each source Checked once its statements are in.
