Bank statement

      
Brings in what the taxi apps paid you and the money going out. Payments from FreeNow, Gett, Uber, Curb and ComCab are recognised, with the commission added back to estimate your fares. Payees are sorted into categories, and anything unknown is left out as personal until you say otherwise. Bank figures replace the expenses you typed, up to the statement's last date.
